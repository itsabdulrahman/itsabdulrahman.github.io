Abdul Rahman

I'm a researcher working on deep learning for medical image computing, with a focus on unsupervised anomaly detection and segmentation. My current work centers on diffusion-based generative models and how the structural priors of medical images (such as bilateral symmetry) can be exploited for robust anomaly localization.

I am the first author of SymmDiff, a symmetry-aware diffusion model for anomaly detection in pelvic X-rays, currently under peer review at MICCAI. I have also reproduced and extended recent diffusion-based anomaly detection methods (e.g. DeCo-Diff) on industrial benchmarks like MVTec-AD and MVTec AD 2.

Outside of research, I co-founded Yumkins, a natural baby-food D2C brand based in Pakistan, where I lead marketing and finance.

Research Interests

Unsupervised anomaly detection (UAD), diffusion models, medical image segmentation, transformer-based architectures, and the use of structural priors (symmetry, pre-trained backbones) to reduce supervision in medical imaging tasks. Benchmarks I work with include MVTec AD, MVTec AD 2, BraTS, and ATLAS v2.
